# Business Insights

## 1. Overall Sales Performance

The dataset contains **128,975 sales records**.

Total revenue generated across all sales was **78,592,678.30**.

The average sales amount per transaction was **648.56**.

---

## 2. Product Category Performance

The **SET** category had the highest total quantity sold.

This indicates that SET products had strong demand based on the number of units sold.

---

## 3. SKU Performance

The analysis identified **7,195 unique SKUs** in the dataset.

The top 5 SKUs were identified based on total quantity sold to understand which individual products had the highest demand.

---

## 4. Monthly Revenue Performance

Monthly revenue was analyzed using the order date.

This helps identify the month with the highest sales revenue and provides insight into seasonal sales patterns.

---

## 5. B2B vs Non-B2B Sales

Sales were divided into:

- B2B transactions
- Non-B2B transactions

This comparison helps understand the contribution of business customers to overall sales.

---

## 6. Fulfilment Analysis

Fulfilment methods were analyzed based on the number of sales.

This helps identify which fulfilment method was used most frequently.

---

## 7. Product Size Analysis

Sales were analyzed by product size to identify which sizes had the highest number of sales.

This can help businesses understand customer preferences and improve inventory planning.

---

## 8. Key Takeaways

- **128,975** sales records were analyzed.
- Total revenue was **78.59M**.
- Average transaction value was **648.56**.
- **7,195** unique SKUs were sold.
- **SET** was the highest quantity-selling category.
- Monthly revenue analysis can help identify sales trends.
- B2B vs Non-B2B analysis provides insight into customer segments.
- Fulfilment and size analysis can support operational and inventory decisions.
